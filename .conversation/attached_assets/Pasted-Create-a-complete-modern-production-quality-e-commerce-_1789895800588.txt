Create a complete, modern, production-quality e-commerce website for an electronics store named **“Prathemesh Electronics.”**

The website should feel like a premium, trustworthy, high-conversion electronics marketplace with the feature depth expected from a large e-commerce platform, while using a completely original visual identity and layout.

## 1. BRAND IDENTITY

Website name:
**Prathemesh Electronics**

Tagline:
**“Powering Your World with Better Technology.”**

Business category:
Electronics, computers, smartphones, accessories, appliances, gaming, smart devices, and technology products.

Create a professional technology-focused brand identity using:

* Modern typography
* Clean spacing
* Premium electronics aesthetic
* Subtle gradients
* Professional icons
* High-quality product imagery
* Responsive layouts
* Smooth animations
* Strong visual hierarchy

Do NOT copy Amazon's exact interface, logo, branding, colors, or proprietary design. Take inspiration only from the functionality expected from a modern large-scale e-commerce website.
